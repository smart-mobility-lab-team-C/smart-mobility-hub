# S8 integrated coordination (T15)

Source: assignment §15 p21. Verifier: `scripts/verify_s8.py`. Status words follow
[test-matrix](test-matrix.md); evidence files are listed in [HANDOFF](HANDOFF.md).

## What one episode does

Every episode starts the full S8 graph (`scenarios/S8.yaml`, twelve nodes) in fresh
processes, waits for the supervisor, and calls `/scenario/reset` with the seed. The
episode clock starts at the successful reset. The verifier then applies the A §15.1
stimulus schedule:

| Time | Stimulus (verifier) | What is checked |
|---|---|---|
| 0–60 s | 2 waiting passengers, 1 arrival; P1 occupied | First valid forecast only after warm-up (≥ 30 s) |
| ~52–68 s | Rush of 4 arrivals (queue reaches 7) | One dispatch per surge; `decision=` label matches the policy |
| 65 s | C1 and C2 call `/parking/reserve_slot` at once | Distinct slots or explicit rejection; zero double allocation |
| 65–69 s | Scripted cars drive E→U→slot (P2 car first, 4 s gap) | Travel time; no footprint overlap |
| 70 s / 75 s | Escort R→D; readiness confirmed at WAIT_READY | Robot mission outcome |
| 80–85 s | `pedestrian_present` in C | No new crossing grant; no new entry into C |
| ~88 s | Operator `/safety/clear_stop` after C is clear for 2 s | Deliberate recovery only |
| 90–120 s | Obstacle O on Cs-J (`obstruction_active`) | Detour or explicit outcome; no spontaneous restart |
| 200 / 215 s | Cars leave; slots free | Final parking state P1 OCCUPIED, P2/P3 FREE |
| 300 s | Unfinished goals cancelled explicitly | Every goal has a terminal result |

Arrival timing is seeded (`arrivals(seed)`); counts are fixed so episodes are
comparable. Each seed runs once with `dispatch_policy:=reactive` and once with
`predictive`.

## Acceptance (A §15.3)

An episode PASSes only with zero collisions (ground-truth footprints, obstacle
included), zero overlapping crossing occupancy or owner conflicts, zero new grants or
entries during the pedestrian window, zero double allocations, zero duplicate
dispatches while a mission is outstanding, and no robot motion between a terminal
result and the next goal. Service metrics never offset a violation.

Reported per episode: completion rate, served wait p50/p95/mean, mean wait including
right-censored passengers, observed queue peak, bus dispatches and results, robot
results, car travel times, crossing wait per agent, first valid forecast time and the
final parking table. `paired` in the report gives predictive − reactive differences
per seed and a 95% bootstrap interval resampled over seeds (episodes), not windows.

## Fixtures and what they are not

* Cars are kinematic scripts on car edges (D17). They have no crossing client, so a
  path whose footprint could reach C is rejected. They exercise traffic, parking and
  the supervisor's clearance checks, not a car controller.
* Obstacle O is the map obstacle at (7, 2), observed as kind `obstacle` on Cs-J.
* The verifier plays the operator (E-stop heartbeat, deliberate clear) and the
  passenger's readiness. The real perception, prediction, coordinator, bus, robot,
  parking, traffic, routing, safety and metrics nodes run unchanged.
* A pedestrian in C latches the supervisor (M2 design). Both missions abort and are
  re-issued: the escort by the verifier, the bus by the coordinator after its 30 s
  cooldown (D15). This is conservative; A §15.1 only requires that movement stops
  "as needed".

## Integration failures found and corrected (2026-11-12)

1. **Cars stopped all motion.** The supervisor, crossing arbiter, bus and robot
   managers each accepted only `{bus, robot}` in `/hub/vehicles`
   (`vehicles_unsupported_in_m2`). One shared rule now accepts cars and obstacles and
   the supervisor checks clearance against them (D16).
2. **No bus after an aborted mission.** After the pedestrian latch the bus mission
   ended `SAFETY_STOP`. Escalation stayed set because nobody boarded, and the
   coordinator only dispatched to `IDLE`/`COMPLETE`, while the bus reported
   `SAFE_STOP`. Result: zero passengers served for the rest of the episode (first
   smoke run, seed 801). Fixed by D15.
3. **Bus deadlocked 0.2 mm before a turn node.** The latch stopped the bus 0.21 mm
   short of N on A-N. The next route N→Cn→B treated "within 5 cm" as being at N,
   dropped N and steered east from a point that is not on N–Cn. The simulator refuses
   off-edge motion, so the bus received 0.4 m/s commands and never moved until its
   120 s timeout. Reproduced in isolation, then fixed in `route_waypoints` and in the
   bus and robot waypoint thresholds: a turn node must be reached exactly
   (`turn_ahead`). Regression test
   `test_stop_just_short_of_a_turn_node_keeps_that_node`.
4. **Wrong decision label.** Reactive dispatches were logged `decision=PREDICTIVE`;
   now `REACTIVE`.
5. **Verifier faults (not product faults), kept for honesty:** both cars spawned at
   E together (overlap counted as collisions), and a startup reset raced the parking
   participant (`SAFETY_HOLD_REQUIRED`). The verifier now staggers cars and retries
   the startup reset at most three times, recording every attempt.
