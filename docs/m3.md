## Authority and runtime policies

Sources: assignment §7 pp10–13, §10 p16, §13 p19, §16 p22 and
§18.3–18.6 pp24–25, from the local PDF/hash in [M1](m1.md).
The user approved the following M3 choices in the implementation plan. They are
choices where the assignment is silent, not newly discovered requirements.

| Policy | Value and behavior |
|---|---|
| Planner transport | 1 s per attempt, initial plus at most two retries; 0.25 s then 0.5 s backoff; zero while waiting; discard expired futures |
| Crossing acquisition | 0.25 s, 0.5 s, then capped 1 s deterministic backoff until the action deadline |
| Crossing renewal | Every 0.25 s; one pending transaction; stop if no acknowledgement for 0.5 s against M2's 1 s lease; retain occupied ownership |
| Final approach | Cruise cap 0.4 m/s; final 1 m cap 0.1 m/s, commanded 0.08 m early to cover command delivery; reduce further near intermediate waypoints; rotate stopped at graph nodes |
| Dock target | B=(8,4,0); ≤0.20 m, ≤0.17 rad, 1 s stable, measured speed <0.02 m/s; command zero before door opening |
| ETA | Time to confirmed docking, excluding boarding; unknown future crossing wait invalid until an observed grant; 1 s exponential speed smoothing; remaining path, slow final metre, turns and hold; unknown waits invalid (-1); reset estimator per goal/reset |
| Edge freshness | 2 Hz snapshots; both source and receipt age ≤1 s; zero future tolerance; unavailable edges removed, no free-road fallback |
| Boarding | Eight seconds from confirmed open; FIFO slots at 2,4,6,8 s, max four per goal; empty slots skipped; new arrivals may fill later slots |
| Cancellation/failure | Immediate zero candidate and close request; confirm fresh zero/closed before canceled; abort with STOP_UNCONFIRMED after 2 s if feedback is lost |

Numeric policies are startup parameters with finite/range validation; source-fixed
docking tolerances/hold and the simulator's 2 s boarding cadence are constants.
The boarding interval is exposed but must equal 8 s; the final-approach distance must equal 1 m. Bus policies and planner freshness are read-only after startup. All clients use monotonic
operation/action deadlines; acquisition/observation stamps use wall-backed ROS time.
No `/clock` is introduced. Passenger `entry_sec` and `boarding_sec` use elapsed
fixed-step simulation time (0.05 s per physics tick); boarding slots follow that
clock. The manager measures the eight-second measured-open door hold with
monotonic wall time. ROS headers retain wall-backed acquisition time. The bus uses a two-thread MultiThreadedExecutor and reentrant action/service
callbacks as assignment §7.3 p13 requires. An RLock protects all shared mission
state; no lock is held across an await. Action execution awaits a goal-specific
Future and never blocks subscription/control callbacks. Other nodes retain their
short serialized callbacks.
The implementation follows the official
[Jazzy action tutorial](https://docs.ros.org/en/jazzy/Tutorials/Intermediate/Writing-an-Action-Server-Client/Py.html)
and installed Jazzy rclpy ActionServer signatures.

## Runtime boundary and interfaces

`ros2 launch smart_hub_bringup m3.launch.py` adds route_planner, bus_manager and
m3_synthetic_roads to M2. The simulator opts into synthetic queue/passenger support.
An external operator must still supply the 10 Hz `/hub/estop` heartbeat. Synthetic
roads are explicit fixture data, not a traffic monitor, perception or evaluation.
Do not run a second publisher on `/hub/queue` or `/traffic/edges` alongside them.
Public M1 schemas, names, rates and QoS remain unchanged.

Internal additions:

- `/sim/bay_clear`: std_msgs/Bool, reliable volatile depth 1, 2 Hz, fixture → bus;
  1 s receipt watchdog, missing/stale means unavailable.
- `/sim/bus_goal_id`: std_msgs/String, reliable volatile depth 1, on transition,
  bus → simulator; empty disables boarding. Only goal correlation, no motion authority.
- `/sim/add_passenger`: std_srvs/Trigger, internal verification client → simulator,
  one immediate synthetic queue arrival at the current simulation time; M3 mode only.
- `/sim/run_id`: std_msgs/String, reliable transient-local depth 1, startup/reset,
  simulator → bus; correlation only.
- `/bus/prepare_reset` and `/bus/finish_reset`: existing ResetScenario type,
  internal reset coordinator → bus.

On approach, crossing acquisition starts at x≥2.5 m on the crossing road
(before the five-metre ETA checkpoint), retaining the upstream stop margin. ETA
requires fresh owned passage while the remaining route intersects inflated C.
After C is behind the entire footprint, no future crossing wait remains.

Routes use Dijkstra and the assignment cost; crossing-wait cost is explicitly zero
because no credible wait estimator exists. Car route-service costs use a declared
0.4 m/s nominal cap only; no car motion is enabled. Bus goals support `bus_id=bus`,
`bay_id=B`, finite positive duration. Busy/unsafe/malformed and initially unreachable goals are rejected (§7.2 p12);
NO_ROUTE from the asynchronous planner after acceptance aborts explicitly. A new explicit goal from a stopped mid-edge pose
recomputes from an adjacent node, validates the connecting current edge, and may
reuse retained bus crossing ownership. This is recovery from a new goal, not
automatic continuation or cached-path execution. A changed or stale active route stops/aborts; dynamic
mid-edge rerouting and the complete S6/T11–T12 acceptance suite remain M6.

Control transforms fresh bus odometry through map→bus/odom, never consumes
`/sim/evaluator/ground_truth`, and observes noisy vehicle/safety/crossing states.
The simulator retains M2 centerline, footprint, final-command expiry, door and
supervisor-loss interlocks. Boarding emits per-person HubEvents with goal/run ID,
queue entry and boarding simulation times. Future arrival schedules never become
online controller inputs. Already boarded passengers survive cancellation until reset.

Reset uses the existing `m2_baseline` scenario ID and public endpoint. M3 enables
bus preparation before M2's hold/crossing/world sequence and bus finish before
releasing safety hold; the total client deadline remains 1 s. Preparation blocks
new goals, terminates any active mission and confirms zero/closed. Partial failure
leaves bus or safety hold in place; retry explicitly. An occupied crossing still
prevents reset. Baseline M2 bringup does not include the bus participant.

colcon tests, M2 regression and real-process M3 probes. Execution results are

blocked bay, stale pose, planner absent/late, no route, timeout, E-stop; door
closure; FIFO/capacity/arrival/interruption/reset; ETA error ≤3 s at 5 m remaining
plus invalid interval duration; route freshness and renewal loss. Save goal,

monitor, robot mission or automatic dispatch is part of M3.

```bash
docker build -t smh-m3 -f containers/Dockerfile .
docker run --rm --user "$(id -u):$(id -g)" -e HOME=/tmp \
  -v "$PWD:/workspace" smh-m3 \
  bash scripts/m3_container.sh /workspace/evidence/runs/UNIQUE_M3_RUN_ID
```

The destination must not exist. The harness retains full traces/logs under the
ignored run directory, parameter dumps for all seven nodes, and case summaries.

closed status. Stale door status yields UNKNOWN with last-known door value and
invalid ETA. Unknown route distance is -1 in feedback; it is never a fabricated zero.
